## Crop

A native macOS menu bar app for recording and capturing your screen. Pick the entire screen, a single window, or a selected portion, then record a video or take a screenshot from a compact floating toolbar, with a timer, microphone and system audio, mouse click highlighting, and a choice of where to save, all without leaving what you are doing.
